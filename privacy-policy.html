Privacy Policy

How we look after the personal information of the parents, carers and young coders who learn with us.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Head Start Coding (“we”, “us”, “our”) runs online coding courses for children aged 8–16. Because we work with young people, we take privacy seriously. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you and your child have under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

This policy is written mainly for parents and carers. There is a short, plain-English version for young people in section 2.

1. Who we are

Head Start Coding is a trading name of Joe Robb, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. Joe Robb is the “controller” of your personal information. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it.

2. For young people: the short version

If you're one of our students, here's what you need to know:

  • We know your first name, your age, and which coding course you're doing. Your parent or carer gave us this when they signed you up.
  • We keep track of your coding projects and progress so we can help you improve and give you your certificates.
  • Sometimes we record lessons so you can catch up if you miss one. We only share recordings with students in your class and their parents, and we delete them when your course or term ends.
  • We never sell your information, and we never send adverts to you.
  • If we were ever worried that you weren't safe, we might have to tell someone who can help, like your parents or a safeguarding professional.
  • You can ask to see the information we have about you, ask us to fix it if it's wrong, or ask us to delete it. Just ask your parent or carer to email us, or email us yourself.
  • Not sure about something? Ask your tutor or a grown-up you trust. There are no silly questions!

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Parent or carer details: your name, email address, phone number and any messages you send us (through our contact forms, email or WhatsApp).
  • Your child’s details: their name, age or age range, the course they’re interested in, their coding experience and interests (for example, from our course quizzes).
  • Booking details: the date and time of a free taster session or lesson you book with us.
  • Learning information: attendance, progress, projects your child creates in lessons, and certificates awarded.
  • Additional needs: any information you choose to share about your child’s learning needs, health or disabilities so we can support them (see section 5).
  • Payment information: records of payments and invoices. Card details are handled securely by our payment provider, Stripe, and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your first name and email address.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit our website, we and our partners collect information about your device and how you use the site, such as pages viewed, links clicked, your approximate location (from your IP address), browser type and the website that referred you. This is collected using cookies and similar technologies. See section 9.

Information from other sources

If you contact us through social media (for example, Facebook or Instagram), we receive the information you share with us there, such as your profile name and message.

Do you have to give us this information?

You don’t have to give us any information. However, we need parent contact details and your child’s name and age to book lessons and to deliver our courses safely. Without them we won’t be able to enrol your child.

5. Sensitive information

Information about health, disabilities or special educational needs is “special category data” and gets extra protection under the law. We only collect it if you choose to tell us, so that we can adapt lessons and support your child properly.

We use it only with your explicit consent, or where it is necessary to safeguard your child’s wellbeing (under the safeguarding of children condition in Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018). We share it only with your child’s tutor and only to the extent needed. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

6. Children’s information

Our students are aged 8–16, so we follow the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) guidance on children’s data and the principles of the Age Appropriate Design Code. In practice:

  • Our website forms are designed for parents and carers, not children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or carer to contact us for you.
  • We collect only the information about your child that we need to teach them well and keep them safe.
  • We never sell children’s data, use it for advertising, or send marketing to children. Our advertising is aimed at adults only.
  • We don’t publish photos, full names or work by students on our website or social media without the parent’s permission (and, where they’re old enough to understand, the child’s agreement too).
  • Children have the same data protection rights as adults. Parents and carers can usually exercise these rights on behalf of their child. Once a child is mature enough to understand their rights (generally around age 12–13 and above), we will take their views into account and may respond to them directly.

Safeguarding

All of our tutors are DBS-checked. If we have a concern about a child’s safety or wellbeing, we may need to record and share information with the relevant authorities (such as children’s social care or the police), sometimes without telling you first, if doing so could put the child at risk. Data protection law does not prevent us from sharing information to keep a child safe.

7. Online lessons and recordings

Our lessons take place on Google Meet. Your child’s first name, voice, and their camera image and screen (if switched on) will be visible to their tutor and to other students in the same group.

Lessons may be recorded so that students who miss a session can catch up, and for safeguarding and quality purposes. Recordings are:

  • only shared with students in the same group and their parents or carers, using a private link;
  • never published publicly or used for marketing;
  • deleted after the period set out in section 12.

Students are welcome to keep their camera off. If you would rather your child is not recorded, just let us know.

8. Marketing and newsletters

We’ll only send you marketing emails (such as coding tips, news and offers) if:

  • you have signed up to our newsletter; or
  • you ticked the optional “send me coding tips, news and offers” box on one of our forms. This box is never pre-ticked, and you can still contact us, take our quiz or download a free guide without ticking it; or
  • you are an existing customer and we’re telling you about our own similar courses. You were given the chance to opt out when you booked, and can opt out at any time.

Emails that you ask for, such as your child’s quiz course recommendation, a free guide you requested, or booking confirmations, are not marketing and are sent to provide the service you requested. We keep a record of when and how you gave consent to marketing so we can show we have it.

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email hello@headstartcoding.co.uk to opt out at any time. Unsubscribing from marketing won’t affect important messages about lessons you have booked.

9. Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as tracking pixels and your browser’s local storage. Some are essential for the website to work; others help us understand how the site is used or measure our adverts. Under PECR, we must ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies, except for limited purposes allowed by law.

When you first visit our website, a banner asks whether you accept analytics and marketing cookies. Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel are not loaded at all unless you say yes, and rejecting them is as easy as accepting. We remember your choice for 12 months and then ask again. 10. Who we share your information with

We never sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Our tutors, who need it to teach your child. They are bound by confidentiality and our safeguarding policies.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, and who may only use your information on our instructions:
    • Netlify (website hosting and contact forms)
    • Zapier (securely passing form submissions between our systems)
    • Google Workspace (Google Meet for live lessons and recordings, plus email, calendar, booking and document storage), and Google Analytics (website analytics)
    • Stripe (card payments)
    • Calendly (taster session booking)
    • MailerLite (newsletter and email delivery)
    • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram advertising measurement and WhatsApp messaging, if you contact us that way)
    • our accountant, for tax and bookkeeping
  • Authorities such as the police, local authority children’s services or HMRC, where we are legally required to, or to protect a child from harm.
  • Professional advisers (e.g. lawyers or insurers) where needed to defend legal claims.
  • A new owner, if our business is ever sold or restructured. We would tell you first.

11. International transfers

Some of our service providers (such as Google, Meta, Stripe, Zapier, Netlify and Calendly) are based in, or store data in, the United States, and MailerLite stores data in the European Economic Area. When your information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected by one of the safeguards allowed under UK law, such as:

  • a UK “adequacy” decision (for example, for the EEA, or US companies certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, known as the “UK–US data bridge”); or
  • the ICO’s International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Contact us if you would like more details about these safeguards.

13. Keeping your information safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including secure (HTTPS) connections on our website, two-factor authentication on our accounts, access restricted to people who need it, private lesson links, and choosing reputable service providers who meet UK data protection standards.

If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours, and tell you without undue delay if there is a high risk to you or your child.

14. Your rights

You (and your child) have the following rights over your personal information:

  • Right to be informed: to know how your data is used, which is what this policy is for.
  • Right of access: to get a copy of the information we hold about you or your child.
  • Right to rectification: to have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
  • Right to erasure: to ask us to delete your information (sometimes called the “right to be forgotten”).
  • Right to restrict processing: to ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Right to data portability: to receive information you gave us in a common digital format, or have it sent to someone else.
  • Right to object: to object to us using your information based on legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Rights relating to automated decisions: not to be subject to decisions made solely by automated means that significantly affect you. (We don’t make any.)
  • Right to withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This won’t affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@headstartcoding.co.uk. It’s free, and we will respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first, and if your request is complex we may extend this by up to two further months; we will tell you if so. Some rights don’t apply in every situation (for example, we can’t delete records we’re legally required to keep), and we’ll explain if that’s the case.

15. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your or your child’s personal information, please tell us first. Email hello@headstartcoding.co.uk with the subject line “Data protection complaint”. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, and let you know the outcome without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator:
